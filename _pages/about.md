My research lies at the intersection of condensed matter theory and quantum information science. I study how symmetry and topology organize quantum phases of matter and enable new approaches to quantum computing. My current work focuses on generalized symmetries, topological orders, and their applications to quantum error correction and fault-tolerant logical operations.

More broadly, I develop theoretical frameworks to classify and characterize quantum phases and understand the transitions between them. My work has spanned crystalline and quasicrystalline topological phases, higher-order topological superconductors, fracton orders, and quantum criticality. 

A full list of my papers is on the [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JkT1DSUAAAAJ&hl=en).
